# 🌻 Happy 21st Birthday, My Sunshine ♡

A dreamy, romantic, fully animated, interactive birthday website dedicated to your boyfriend's 21st birthday. Designed with a soft golden-hour sunflower theme, vintage scrapbook aesthetics, paper textures, and a deeply heartfelt 2,000-word love letter celebrating the journey of how your best friend became your boyfriend.

---

## ✨ Features Included

1. **The Opening Surprise Envelope**
   - Creamy golden-hour sky with floating petals and glowing stars.
   - 3D animated envelope with a sunflower wax seal.
   - Unfolds smoothly with a burst of pastel confetti and storybook transition.

2. **The Main Hero Section**
   - Illustrated sunflower bouquet, glowing birthday cake preview, and twinkling stars.
   - Heading: *"HAPPY 21ST BIRTHDAY, MY SUNSHINE ♡"*
   - Animated handwritten note and smooth scroll indicator.

3. **Interactive Sunflower Garden**
   - Sunflowers gently sway in the breeze and dynamically tilt their heads toward the mouse cursor.
   - Fluttering butterflies and bumblebees.
   - Clickable sunflowers that reveal hidden handwritten love notes in popups.

4. **The Love Letter**
   - A shorter handwritten-style letter on vintage parchment, organized into ten sections:
     - **A:** Our Beginning — When you were my best friend
     - **B:** How much you truly mean to me
     - **C:** My favorite person in the entire universe
     - **D:** The little things I adore about you
     - **E:** How deeply proud I am of you (turning 21, patience with yourself)
     - **F:** My wishes for your 21st year (peaceful mornings, real fulfillment, courage)
     - **G:** I want the world to be kind to you
     - **H:** Our memories and our future
     - **I:** What I need you to always remember (worth is not tied to productivity/money)
     - **J:** All my love, today and always (the grand closing)

5. **21 Reasons You're My Sunshine**
   - Exactly 21 scrapbook cards for 21 years!
   - Each card reveals a thoughtful, sincere reason with gentle hover effects and celebratory sparkles.

6. **Interactive 3D Greeting Card**
   - Realistic 3D folding physical-style card with pressed flowers and wax seal.
   - Folds open on click with an inside handwritten note.
   - Built-in **"Print / Save as PDF 📄"** button to export a real physical keepsake!

7. **Birthday Cake & 21 Candle Wish**
   - Storybook illustrated cake with strawberries, whipped cream, and 21 glowing candles.
   - Click candles to toggle flames individually.
   - **"Make a Birthday Wish 🎂"** button dims the cake, intensifies the flames, gently blows them out with smoke puffs, and bursts confetti + floating balloons while revealing a touching blessing.

8. **Party Popper Celebration**
   - **"CELEBRATE MY BOY'S 21ST!! 🎉"** button triggers a full-screen canvas celebration with pastel confetti, golden stars, floating hearts, and balloons.

9. **Soft Background Music Player**
   - Floating sunflower player with play/pause and volume control.
   - **Soundtrack:** "mine<3" (`assets/audio/mine.mp3`) plays without a video embed.

10. **"If I Could Give You The World" Section**
    - 10 interactive clickable clouds with heartfelt future wishes.
    - Concludes with: *"i can't give you the whole world, my love. but i can always wish for it to be kinder to you."*

11. **Timeline of Our Story**
    - Vertical timeline titled *"from best friends to my favorite kind of love ♡"* with sunflower nodes and customizable milestones.

12. **The Open-When Letters**
    - 6 digital envelopes with wax seals:
      - Open when you're having a bad day
      - Open when you're doubting yourself
      - Open when you miss me
      - Open when you need a little motivation
      - Open when you want to remember how loved you are
      - Open when you need to smile

13. **The Secret Surprise**
    - Hidden glowing sunflower button: *"psst... one last little thing ♡"*
    - Reveals a secret love letter with your inside jokes list and special nicknames.

14. **The Final Birthday Message**
    - Sunset golden-hour closing with the prompt's heartfelt text and handwritten signature: *"made with all my love, just for you. — your nautanki 🌻"*
    - **"replay our little story ♡"** button to restart the experience.

## 🚀 How to Run & View Locally

1. **Run with a local web server** (needed for YouTube music playback):
   - If you have Python:
     ```bash
     python -m http.server 3000
     ```
     Then open [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000) in your browser.
   - Or with Node.js:
     ```bash
     npx serve
     ```
   - Opening `index.html` directly may prevent YouTube from playing inside the page; the music player will offer a link to open the video on YouTube.

## 🌐 How to Publish Online for Free

You can host this website online in less than 2 minutes so you can text him the link on his birthday:

### Option A: Netlify Drop (Fastest — 30 seconds)
1. Go to [https://app.netlify.com/drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop).
2. Drag and drop the entire project folder (`ashu`) onto the page.
3. Done! You will get a live link like `https://sunshine-birthday-21.netlify.app`.

### Option B: GitHub Pages
1. Create a free GitHub repository.
2. Upload the files and enable GitHub Pages in **Settings > Pages > Deploy from Branch (main)**.
3. Your website is live at `https://yourusername.github.io/ashu`.
